## What we publish

- City pages with public courts and recreation-center drop-in sessions that we could verify on an official website.
- Guides that explain open-play etiquette and first equipment in plain language.
- Source links and a last-checked date on every venue, so you can see where the facts came from.

## How listings get on the site

A venue is added only after we read an official city or county parks page, or the venue's own website. We record the address, hours, fees, and beginner notes exactly as those pages state them, or we say that a detail was not published.

We do not scrape or copy listings from third-party court directories, map apps, or social networks. If we cannot verify a court, we leave it off.

Hours, fees, and reserved-court rules change. Treat this site as a starting point, then confirm on the official page or the sign at the gate.
