This program is for pickleball coaches and instructors anywhere in the United States. If you recommend [AIKAI](https://aikai.co) paddles to your students, you are assigned one numbered promo code when you join, from COACH-01 through COACH-10. Your matching link uses the Shopify discount format, https://aikai.co/discount/COACH-XX, with your number in place of XX.

Your students get {{COACH_DISCOUNT}} on all AIKAI products. The code cannot be combined with holiday pricing or other promotions. You earn {{COACH_COMMISSION}} made with your code, once the order is completed and unreturned. You also receive {{REWARD_PADDLE}} (to use as a demo or to sell) after three completed, unreturned sales plus a testimonial posted on the AIKAI site or wherever AIKAI shows up in search, for example a Google review.

## How it works

1. Request a code by email (details below). When you join, you are assigned one unused code from COACH-01 through COACH-10, plus the matching link (for example, [https://aikai.co/discount/COACH-01](https://aikai.co/discount/COACH-01)).
2. Share your code or link with students and players you coach. Tell them the code cannot be combined with holiday pricing or other promotions.
3. You earn {{COACH_COMMISSION}} (completed, unreturned orders only).
4. After three qualifying sales and a testimonial posted on the AIKAI site or wherever AIKAI shows up in search, you also receive {{REWARD_PADDLE}} to use as a demo or to sell.

## FAQ

**What counts as a referral?** A completed, unreturned order that used your coach code or matching link. Canceled or returned orders do not count toward the three-sale paddle reward or toward the $15 payment.

**How does the $15 payment work?** You earn {{COACH_COMMISSION}} made with your code, after the order is completed and unreturned. Payments are made after the return window. The payout method and schedule are confirmed when you join.

**How is the free paddle earned and delivered?** After three qualifying sales and a testimonial posted on the AIKAI site or wherever AIKAI shows up in search, we contact you and ship {{REWARD_PADDLE}} to a US address. You may use it as a demo or sell it. Shipping is within the United States only. A short review from you is enough. A Google review counts, as does a review on the AIKAI site.

**Can I keep referring after I earn a paddle?** Yes. You can keep sharing your code. You continue to earn {{COACH_COMMISSION}} after that. Additional free-paddle rewards, if any, will be announced. We are not promising more paddles at this time.

**Do I need to tell my students?** Yes. If you share your code or link, tell students that you receive a reward when they buy with it. That is required under FTC endorsement guides. A simple line such as "I earn a reward if you buy with my code" is enough. Students get {{COACH_DISCOUNT}}. The code cannot be combined with holiday pricing or other promotions.
