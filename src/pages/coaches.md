This program is for pickleball coaches and instructors anywhere in the United States. If you recommend [AIKAI](https://aikai.co) paddles to your students, you are assigned one numbered promo code when you join, from COACH-01 through COACH-10. Your matching link uses the Shopify discount format, https://aikai.co/discount/COACH-XX, with your number in place of XX.

Your students get {{COACH_DISCOUNT}} on all AIKAI products. You earn both rewards: {{COACH_COMMISSION}} made with your code, once the order is completed and unreturned, and {{REWARD_PADDLE}} after your first three completed, unreturned sales.

## How it works

1. Request a code by email (details below). When you join, you are assigned one unused code from COACH-01 through COACH-10, plus the matching link (for example, [https://aikai.co/discount/COACH-01](https://aikai.co/discount/COACH-01)).
2. Share your code or link with students and players you coach.
3. You earn {{COACH_COMMISSION}} (completed, unreturned orders only).
4. After your first three qualifying sales, you also receive {{REWARD_PADDLE}}.

Email [jay@aikai.co](mailto:jay@aikai.co?subject=AIKAI%20coach%20referral) with:

## FAQ

**What counts as a referral?** A completed, unreturned order that used your coach code or matching link. Canceled or returned orders do not count toward the three-sale paddle reward or toward commission.

**How does the commission work?** You earn {{COACH_COMMISSION}} made with your code, after the order is completed and unreturned. Commissions are paid out after the return window. The payout method and schedule are confirmed when you join.

**How is the free paddle delivered?** After your first three qualifying sales, we contact you and ship {{REWARD_PADDLE}} to a US address. Shipping is within the United States only.

**Can I keep referring after I earn a paddle?** Yes. You can keep sharing your code. You continue to earn {{COACH_COMMISSION}} after that. Additional free-paddle rewards, if any, will be announced. We are not promising more paddles at this time.

**Do I need to tell my students?** Yes. If you share your code or link, tell students that you receive a reward when they buy with it. That is required under FTC endorsement guides. A simple line such as "I earn a commission if you buy with my code" is enough. Students get {{COACH_DISCOUNT}}.
